December 6, 2025 - Session Notes

Dec 06, 202511 min read

  • DnD

In camp with Breeze Rafross Gladys and Anwynn

  • Breeze has gone into the camp on the other side of the wall, and it trying to get information about the four gnomes that we found are traitors.
  • We pass Elga off to Gleeson, Breeze sits down with three guards and describes the gnomes to them, 1 photorealistic drawing, one good impression, and two that are not much help at all.
  • Gladys sees Ivir and Costa coming in to camp, and while motherly and caring, seeing that Ivir has taken someone under his wing, tells Ivir that her priority is her people and her family, and what’s happening with the human kingdom. Her lowest priority is investigating the Tabaxi further.
  • Gladys excuses herself and breaks off to be with her family and people.
  • Ivir leaves Costa at a medical tent, he lies down next to his bed and rests.
  • Rafross, Breeze and Anwynn are completely exhausted and collapse.
  • Before collapsing, there’s a lingering thought on each mind, for Ivir his thoughts linger on old dreams and anger and frustration. Annwyn thinks about what Kisha said about being a fragment of her brother, playing over and over the images of the battle she caused. Rafross think of scattered flashback to staring into the void, but falls asleep without being tormented too much. Breeze thinks on his fear and concern and the compicated feeling of his home.
  • Breeze dreams about the water and trees and greenery of his wetland home, running through the landscape with his brother, standing in the freshwater and looking around, and seeing the wetlands extend forever. Where the mountains should be there’s an endless expanse of water strething into the distance. He turns and notices his brother is no longer there. The water is no longer still, and the water starts shifting and turning and boiling with streams of water chaotically twisting. He watches as the water boils away and the land dries, his eyes are blinded by smoke, his final thought is of falling backwards into the boiling water, deeper than the water ever was, and he is embraced by the calm darkness and falls into a restful sleep.
  • Ivir falls asleep feeling shame, anger and frustration, the feeling of the primal giant magic simultaneously with unregulated power of holy fire. His thoughts are on a change of the core of his being, the recently taken oaths that have given him so much meaning with his new companions, the way his rage has become more and more useful. His conflicted feelings about the loss of the gentle inocence he used to embody, contrasted against how much utility his anger is finding. He falls into a shallow sleep, travelling through an ambiguous forrested landscape, he looks down and in a mirror of a dream, he has his flaming glaive in his hand, where the dream he remembers was not through his eyes. He knows that in the real world the fire doesn’t harm him, but in this dream all the shame and anger he feels flows out from the glaive as fire, burning him, chipping away at everything he’s feeling, until he’s hollowed out? relaxed? he can’t say.
  • Anwynn drifts into sleep, dreaming of a time they shouldn’t remember. But seems to remember some anyway, having now been told. Anwynn, the small, intangible, essence, stripped of any embodiment, travelling through a space where only that part can exist. Feeling tethered by lifelines extending into the fog. The strongest link thinking of what they’ve been told about where they come from, contrasted against the horrors of their sibling Kisha, how they lack in everything that Kisha uses for horror. Thinking about Sam, who’s been with them longer than any other body, the connections to the other companions accompanied with a sense of peace and reassurance, realising that these bonds they have made are just as strong. They feel a sense of conviction, that regardless of what form they may take, in whatever realm, they can’t let there be any horrors like are done in the name of Kisha flow from them. Pulling on the strongest of all bonds, with their patron, they feel the link warp and shift, becoming a weapon, the weight of conviction. They drift into sleep.
  • Rafross is the last to fall asleep. Defying comprehension he sees images of many eyes, things in the darkness he will never understand. But beneath the surface, he has more wisdom than others can see. And as he’s falling asleep with all of these faces? shapes? constructs? and with that wisdom, he can tell that next time he’s confronted with this, he’ll be able to understand it more. With that feeling, as he drifts into a deeper sleep, he feels a weight wrap around his wrists, settle on his body, and bind him. The visions and eyes are on the periphery, watching but not a threat, but there is something else. He feels that weight, he feels an offer as the pressure on his hands pulses and lifts. The weight on the rest of his body lifts, but he feels that iron offer of power in his fists. He faces this with curiosity, reaching out towards the source of the offer of power with his consciousness to try and know more about what these feelings are. It doesn’t feel the same as the hand wraps, not soft, but like dried mud or rock, the feeling of his body hardening, becoming stronger if less flexible and dexterous. He has a feeling of being welcomed back, it feels familiar but without knowing why. He rolls a 19 perception and it feels recent, something he’s bereft of presently but has known, but can’t tell exactly what it is. It would feel good to take, but his wisdom holds him back from grasping it immediately, but he’s held in place till he makes a choice. In the end, not being allowed to be indecisive, so he reaches out and walks through the door, he sinks down into the ground beneath him, into its solidity, beneath the earth he breaths deep and finally rests.

The Next Day

  • Costa stirs a little when Ivir wakes, but quickly falls back asleep again, needing far more rest.
  • We gather and get updated and debriefed.
  • The guards have gone through the the camp but didn’t find any of the four faces, but there are others still that are also missing and unaccounted for.
  • The other leaders Steve and Aiden are antsy about Elga being locked up, but have little ability to do anything as this is in the guards hands.
  • Ivir wants to talk to Elga, but Heinrich is wary of letting him near her. Ivir tries to convince him to let them talk, but isn’t able to win him over.
  • Anwynn has gone over the other side of the wall and retrieved enough warforged parts to construct a full body. Feeling very uncomfortable with relying on other people, they try to leave Sams body, but Sam doesn’t feel the same way. She’s enjoyed this feeling of usefulness, of capability, and doesn’t want that to end yet. They agree to stay together like this for the time being, but Anwynn deliberately steps back and lets Sam have this time for herself, to be with the other soldiers, to help with the construction of camp.
  • Rafross uses this time to attempt to decipher the scroll.
  • Breeze spends time using sending to communicate messages, one to Emdi, and another to his mother as an olive branch. He’s troubled, but spends his time playing music to the children in camp, lifting the spirits of the refugees who have been terrified. He feels echoes of the dream from the previous night in bringing calm and joy to the people here. The feeling of helping community and reaching out to help people is a deep instinct that he hasn’t always been able to do. But as he plays he finds that his music is incredibly effective.
  • Ivir manages to talk to Elga, and wants to ask what could make her abandon her people like this: “I asked you to put your faith in me, and I put all my faith into you, it could have benefited everyone, and yet you betrayed that and I have no idea how you could do that.”
  • He manages to get her talking, less in a tempting way, but pushing and pushing and getting a rise out of her. The mask of indifference slips a little as the power and rightness of Kisha is sleighed: “Why are you so convinced I’ve abandoned my people?? I haven’t abandoned my people! I’m leading them to salvation.”
  • Ivir: “This power you’ve put your faith in, obviously isn’t all powerful, they couldn’t even squash us.”
  • She responds that “If she wanted to she could have”, “She had a reason for letting you live”, “This is a narrative she’s creating, she’s sewing the field for what she wants to create”
  • Ivir responds “Is that just soil for tilling? All those people and lives and bodies”
  • And she feels that of course that’s true, there must be change, the forces of the feywild that withdrew and her people are a dying plant, that can no longer be saved, and that the ground needs to be tilled and renewed.
  • The scroll that Rafross found is: The immaterial nature of material magic. Talks about artificers and their inability to cast magic with no material components, even those that wizards can do. Talks about the necessity of material components and loci. Talks about how artificers excel in the ability of creating permanent magic items. Rest of the scroll is dedicated to cataloguing unique and rare magical items, sheaths of paper that have been collected and bundled together, the original collection of all of this knowledge. Some are single pages but some are dense bundles of paper. Mechanically this acts as a ritual scroll of identify. Notes include copius notes speculating on items creations. It has obviously been opened and reworked and added to over time. The entry for the handwraps is a heavy bundle, but the entry in common is small, and all the other pages are in an unrecognised language. Wraps are posed as the material component of a binding spell. There are attempted translations of another even older text that isn’t there. The symbol of Kisha is present and is translated as “fundamental material”.
  • Chris reading from the page Sarah-Jane passed over: A text with many attempted by translations by many people, hard to say how much is allegory and how much is real, a repetition of line similar to what we found in the underwater temple. Two entities seemed to have combined to flood something else with primordial waters. Talk of the Abyss. A quadrant diagram with some symbols that aren’t recognised.

A textual interpretation of the page that Sarah-Jane sent through:

To the still [Deep-waters] Apsu, came his other half [wife, partner, or lit. ‘opposite’], the boiling [Chaotic-movement] Ummu-Hubur [Mother-River, -watercourse, ass. w. netherworld].

The [Mother river] boiled so, and she and [the Deep Waters - Apsu/Abzu?] combined and flooded the great Abzu [Cosmic Abyss, or lit. deep waters-Sea] with the primordial waters from which sprung the twin-horizons [ -gods, -principles]. Kishar [the whole earth, horizon of the earth] and Anshar [the whole sky, horizon of the sky]

As two-halves [partners] they drew their power inward, and the [Deep-waters + Mother-river] flowed outwards. Between them, the twin-horizons became the cradle of life.

  • As Rafross and Ivir are looking through the bundles, hunting for information, they also look for any information about anything similar to Ivir’s glaive. Closest thing they find first is something called a flame tongue. Similar symbolism. References to Hir-Tia have few references to weapons of any kind. Something is mentioned that items being in the presence of powerful magic, like dragons, can come to take on the attributes of the magic they’re surrounded by. A theory that exceptionally strong items can synergise with their users to amplify the powers of both.
  • As Rafross is showing the scroll to Gleeson, we suddenly hear the noise of people riding into camp, accompanied by a wave of conversation and noise.
  • Gleeson turns suddenly mid conversation, and for the first time looking like an actual military officer, moves very quickly towards the source of the noise.
  • The army column splits in two, and Serath and Marcella ride forward.
  • Sereth, supporting herself with Ivirs hand, lithely steps down from her horse, salutes Gleeson, and nods to the group before standing by as Marcella dismounts.
