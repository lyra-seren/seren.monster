Pre-session

Players present:

Recapping some of the events from last session.

Ivir Talking with Sereth

  • Sereth asks Ivir if he had to choose between what was best for his village, and what his god wanted, what would he do? Ivir responds immediately that he would choose whats best for his village, gods have shown themselves to be small minded and cruel and not necessarily any better, and that what matters is the actual people, here and now. Sereth responds that for her, Marcella is her village, she is not under her orders, but the people under her are under that chain of command. Sereth doesn’t have a god in the same way that Ivir does, but she has oaths and commitments to the people around her and she places her faith in Marcella because she is what’s good for her people.
  • Ivir plunges his glaive into the ground in front of him, and asks Sereth to promise him, that if Marcella ever starts acting against the interest of Sereth’s people, that she won’t continue following Marcella.
  • Sereth says that she will never doubt Mareclla, she looks at the glowing burning glaive, and looks at it with unreadable intensity, Ivir rolls a 7 perception.
  • Sereths sword is drawn, and Ivir reaches out his hand and grips the blade, letting it cut into his hand.
  • Looking directly at Ivir, she places her hand over his on her blade, and promises him that she will stand alongside him in defence of the values they both hold. But that she cannot promise to break the oaths she has already sworn to Marcella, and that the trust and faith she has in Marcella is greater than Ivir has had time to see or understand.
  • Ivir thanks her for his honesty.
  • She gently moves her sword to prompt Ivir to remove his hand, bleeding and cut.
  • Ivir swings his hand to flick off the blood, and while doing so uses lay on hands to heal his hand. As he does so his hand glows hot, and the drops of blood smoke as they hit the ground.
  • As she sees this, she glances down at his blood on her sword, and has an internal thought that Ivir is unable to parse.
  • Standing alone in the circle of light being cast by his glaive, Ivir thinks on the oaths that matter to him, the oaths to his god, and his village in people, but also the found family, and the new connections he’s forged. Swearing not to his god, or any other power, but just to himself he makes these promises to himself.
  • Rolls religion check, with charisma bonus and proficiency, 19.
  • Thinking of the wilds protecting, this strong conviction, he smells the scent of smoke, remembers the dream and the power of his rage. The glaive suddenly flashes with intense bright frames, illuminating the forest around him, and then dissipates completely. As he turns to walk back to camp, he notices that he’s standing in the centre of a large burnt out pit. Having come to terms with his anger, and come to a place where it can be used without danger, he calmly turns to go back to camp.

Marcella Talking to Anwynn

  • Bustles in to Anwynn’s tent, very quickly filling the room with conversation, talking about how Anwynn’s going to need a much better face for this coming job.
  • Introduces Jamie Lavender, one of the current ambassadors with a lot of diplomatic experience.
  • Anwynn and Sam talk internally, and decide that while some small part of Sam has been enjoying spending time amongst her people, she’s been finding enormous value in being able to do good in the world.
  • They decide together that one way or another Sam will come with them to the human kingdom.
  • Anwynn talks to Jamie and he seems to be sincere. Long time collaborator and confidant of Marcella, and knows what he’s signing up for.
  • He agrees to travel with us, even if he’s not being the host for Anwynn immediately.
  • Anwynn politely declines Marcella’s offer for everyone to leave via teleportation circle in the middle of the night, perceiving her wish for things to be discrete, and forces her hand into letting the party leave at dawn.
  • Marcella is obviously annoyed at having lost the uncritical cooperation of Anwynn and quickly leaves.

Rafross Looking for Monastery Contacts

  • Rafross is looking for information, talking to anyone he can, trying to find any of his contacts so he can make contact.
  • The name Edith is mentioned and recognised, but not any others.
  • Hopes that asking about their names will get back to them somehow and they’ll know that Rafross is looking to contact them.

Before Dawn Departure

  • As Rafross is returning to our camp, he sees someone who obviously recognises him, it’s Jamie.
  • Rafross rolls perception on meeting Jamie, looking for anything that might look out of place, or something out of place or unexpected.
  • Get’s 28: Clearly in travelling clothes, ready to leave at any time; lots of hidden weapons hidden all over his body; a very deliberate nondescript appearance, with culturally neutral and unassuming clothing; very pleasant to talk to and reasonably sounding.
  • Ivir goes to talk to his bears, Misha, Ivan, and Boris; then walks with them to the medical tent where Costa is resting.
  • Screams are heard from the medical tent upon the bears walking in, one stern nurse tells him clearly that the bears must behave. Ivir agrees and hunkers down with the bears to sleep.
  • Breeze and Gladys come back to camp, and are brought up to date on everything that’s happening.
  • In the morning Jamie starts moving around the camp waking up everyone for departure.
  • Ivir is woken gently by the bears waking, noticing that Costa is talking to the stern nurse.
  • Costa is sitting up, putting on clothes, with a pack at the ready.
  • Costa insists that he won’t let Ivir leave him alone again.
  • Ivir hoists him up in the air by his pack, and Misha immediately steps underneath him so Costa can ride.
  • Ivir tries to pick him up again and drop him on the ground, but Misha catches him again.
  • Ivir tries to convince both of them that he can’t let them come with them and risk them getting killed.
  • Costa insists that he can’t stay here and that he needs to go back to the human empire.
  • Ivir reluctantly agrees that he can come, and Misha is 100% committed to protecting Costa.
  • Costa excitedly notices Jamie, the only other human.
  • Marcella hands the party a small mirror that can locate shards if they’re within 1000 feet, and that it’s either a large chunk of the crystal, or many all in the same place. They have scried the location, and know that it succeeded, but all they can see is darkness.
  • She also hands a diplomatic letter requesting an audience and peaceful reception to Jamie.
  • The teleportation circle is confirmed to be working and can send someone back. It’s located in a abandoned dwarven city, thus located underground, but everything is structurally sound.
  • We step through the circle of teleportation and into the caverns.
