March 2, 2025 Session Notes

March 2, 2025 - Session-Notes

Mar 02, 20254 min read

  • DnD

Ivir, Gladys, and Rafross

  • Ivir and Rafross tell Gleeson what’s over the hill and what kind of danger they’re in.
  • Gleeson immediately arcs up and pushes back on being told what to do.
  • Ivir tries again to warn of the danger and tells him he’s wasting valuable time, succeeds after intimidation check.
  • Turns to address Gladys specifically and says that he’s willing to negotiate for protection for the refugees outside the gate in return for us fighting with them to fend off the attack.
  • Gleeson invites the leaders to dinner to discuss the agreement.
  • Gleeson orders Heinrich to get his men to collect the other leaders, but obviously doesn’t feel confident in his authority.
  • Ivir and Rafross clock that as much as Gleeson is pissed off he’s holding all his magic back because he realises that it’ll be needed more later. Rafross additionally realises that while moving through the fortress Gleeson is sending a lot of message spells, and that he’s setting a lot up that depends on getting our help.

Annwyn and Breeze

  • Both have to make a luck roll to see how much trouble we’re in with a tabaxi strolling up to the main gate.
  • Two wood elves come up and demand answers, just as I finish saying “I know this looks bad but does he look like a soldier???” Breeze dimension doors both of us into the room with everyone else, with Rafross’ hand wraps as a teleportation target.
  • Breeze mentions “Marcelas bears” and gets punched by Ivir immediately for 5 damage.
  • Annwyn tells Gleeson about the warforged and the religiously motivated army and that they’re disconnected from most of the Tabaxi.
  • While this is all happening, Breeze draws the Gaia symbol and compares it to the medallion we got from the assassin and we realise it’s the same.
  • Rafross gets a 28 perception and realises he has a scroll from our research in the city into the religious symbols found on the underwater statue, and realises its the same language.
  • Breeze and I work together using this information to try and decipher what the writing on the Gaia symbol says, previously we had deciphered “Rest here Kisha in the embrace/prison of Abzul” Kisha is symbol of all earth and is related to Gaia/is the same as Gaia.
  • For Gleeson to agree to let the refugees in, he needs us to agree to tear down the barrier.
  • He’s done a number of experiments on the barrier, non-magical people can walk through but inevitably find themselves lost and confused and turned around. Lesser wizards can push through a little but with great resistance. Gleeson was able to push far enough to approach a physical barrier, but he is no longer able to describe what he saw or felt, which means it can manipulate even his mind.
  • Gleeson agrees to allocate an officer to oversee the refugees coming into the fort immediately, and Gleeson takes us down a secret passage towards the barrier so that we can start work on bringing it down immediately.
  • Annwyn pulls mask out. Mask of many faces. Casts contact other plane.

Annwyn: Do you get along well with other divine entities? Patron: Sometimes yes, sometimes no

Annwyn: Do you feel this? (Annwyn steps forward into the barrier) Patron: … (Can’t get a response properly, steps back)

Annwyn: Do you want to help tear this barrier down? Patron: Perhaps, I could help empower your magic, or you could open another way.

  • Pull out doorknob and start experimenting with it.

Meanwhile the others continue moving forward:

  • Rafross starts getting hard to see/focus on and is far ahead, but continues going. becomes engulfed in darkness.
  • Others start facing enormous resistance.
  • Breeze casts dancing lights to see if they can penetrate into the darkness. The dancing lights are resisting Breeze’s directions to move forward, takes an action instead of a bonus action.
  • Rafross tells Breeze that he’s pulling on some kind of invisible rope that’s guiding him.
  • Breeze keeps trying to move but every step forwards somehow ends up taking him backwards.
