Recap

  • From fire was unable to enter any of the places I needed to go.
  • My Patron did some weird shit and infuriatingly insisted on the 5 questions format even when not needed.
  • Was told that I needed to go and help my friend, but confusingly my patron doesn’t care even remotely about my friends.
  • Asked how I can meet with my patron in person and was given a door knob and a mask.
  • Was taken to Breeze’s village where he had been teleported and immediately gotten in to trouble and ran up a tree.
  • Helped smooth things over with Breeze’s tabaxi family, and told his mother about what happened with his brother. He doesn’t know.
  • We were sent to the front line where we found out that the big fire that was being blamed by both elves and tabaxi on the other side, was actually an enormous robot army of unknown allegiance.

Today’s Session

Anwynn and Breeze

  • Breeze and I continuing to travel with the tabaxi towards the front lines, hoping to get some answers out of them and observe the warforged, before losing them and crossing the lines during the night.
  • Get taken by (Shadow of the) Hunter and (Hunters) Mark to meet with a tabaxi in the dress of the Gaia clergy.
  • Supported Breeze in trying to trick the tabaxi.
  • Succeeded a deception check to explain away why I have a cloak her recognised as being worn by another in his order.
  • Succeeded in getting Breeze to not do anything stupid.

Ivir and Rafross

  • Ivir makes a whole lotta noise using Thaumaturgy to attempt to call to all the animals charging at the enemy.
  • Some scouts from the human kingdom jumped out of the trees attempting to stop him.
  • The leader of the scouts agree to give two of his number to watch over the army of warforged overnight while the rest go with Ivir to convince the elves to let those fleeing through the wall.

Gladys

  • Has finally made it to her village and her people.
  • Is welcomed to a ragtag group of leaders, representing all the different familys/groups in the camp.
  • Aiden is a hothead wood elf who is just angry and aggressive about being locked out of the wall, and is upset at Gladys for not seemingly doing enough. Is intimidated into silence after being questioned for what he’s actually done.
  • No one knows why they haven’t been granted passage.
  • Stefan has spoken to military people in charge of the wall but have just delayed and not said anything concrete.
  • Discussing how to force their hand to get passage for the people in the camp.
  • Helga a forest gnome who seems very cool. Ripped grandma. “Lets go give them a piece of our mind why don’t we”.
  • Gladys and the others go up to those in charge of the wall to demand answers.
  • Helga follows Gladys in sitting on a crate in the middle of a walkway to protest being stonewalled on information.
  • Others start join in their sit in protest.
  • Gladys weaponises the small children of her family to bother the guards.
  • Gladys pulls rank and gets taken inside to talk to the general (Heinrich).
  • General Heinrich parrots a very rehearsed line about “processing times” and “due processes” that must be followed before any refugees will be allowed in.
  • Realises that none of the replies to the bureaucratic sendings have been received. This is a surprise to him.
  • He goes to talk to the wizard and asks Gladys to wait.

At this time it’s the end of the day, Rafross and Ivir are coming close to the wall and Gladys again. Anywnn and Breeeze are settling down to camp before sneaking away.

Back to Breeze and Anywnn

  • Breeze asks again if we should escape.
  • Plan to dimension door from the tent off to the side to get as far away from the army as possible.
  • Get a 20+8 on deception to leave a Gaia symbol on the floor of the tent, succeeding in having them not immediately chase us.
  • Run into the scouts that were working with Ivir and convince them that we’re trusted. They realise we know Ivir.

All Coming together Again

  • Rafross runs ahead of Ivir to watch the chaos unfold.
  • Ivir, accompanied by a stampede of all the animals of the forest, led in stride by 4 large bears, thunder directly into the middle of the refugee camp.
  • Gladys sitting in the waiting room.
  • A wizard comes to talk to Gladys and is imperious and snobby and rude, takes 2 psychic damage when Gladys assets herself as an agent of Marcella.
  • Heinrich speaks on Gladys’ behalf as well to demand answers from the Wizard.
  • In the distance Gladys begins to hear Ivirs chanting and the sound of animals stampeding.
  • Ivir halts his army of animals, and walks authoritatively with his four bears towards the gate.
  • The sit in flees further inside in the face of the stampede.
  • Ivir demands for everyone in the camp to be let in, or to talk to the leader of all elves immediately. Guard is incredibly flustered but tries to agree to let him talk to the wizard.
  • The bears have to stay outside.
  • Rafross stealthily climbs up the wall and slips into a window, quickly making himself seem right at home.
  • Obnoxious wizard has an aneurysm at the sight of all the animals.
  • Rafross, Gladys, and Ivir together manage to talk Wizard Gleeson into being honest.
  • Gleeson finally tells the truth at last and apparently the elven valley is completely inaccessible and it’s not possible for them to enter the valley, or send magic in or out.
