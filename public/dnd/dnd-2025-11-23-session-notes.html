November 23, 2025 Session Notes

November 23, 2025 - Session Notes

Nov 23, 20257 min read

  • DnD

Recap

  • Fought and beat Kisha/Gaia.
  • Fought a big defensive war against the oncoming army, evacuating all the refugees to the other side of the wall.
  • Took out almost all enemies, but two large seige engines are standing but inoperable.
  • Wall was almost entirely destroyed following a BETRAYAL BY ELGA, who opened the gate mid battle.

Pursuing Elga through the keep

  • Needing to peruse Elga to ensure that she doesn’t return to the tabaxi and ruin Breeze’s cover story.
  • Annwyn used psychic lance which gave an indication of what direction she was.
  • Settle on a plan to have Gladys and Rafross wait near the exit to spot her if she leaves.
  • While they’re waiting, Annwyn and Breeze wait sneakily to spot her moving.
  • Ivir feels furious at the betrayal, and letting go any restraint, we see him gain a glowing red aura, and the glaive has iron flakes fall off, and it takes on a cracked magma.
  • Kisha’s words about “my mothers rage” spring to mind.
  • Ivir is using divine sense/detect magic to search for Elga, his vision has gone hazy red, in his vision of red he sees a ghostly blue shape moving through on the other side of the wall, not an extraplanar being, but there are extraplanar objects in the same area.
  • Ivir, despite knowing that there’s a wall between him and the blue shape, explosively rages and attempts to power straight through the wall and any obstacles.
  • Crits on attack role, and the wall is obliterated to molten rock by the magical red glowing glaive as he charges straight through.
  • Immediately upon entering that room he’s blasted with some kind of gritty poisonous something, that he manages to not get effected by.
  • Charges right up to where Elga is, though she’s in cover.
  • Attacks the rock she’s taking cover behind and destroys the cover, but doesn’t hit her at all.
  • She runs and lays traps.
  • We pursue, managing to pick up her tracks after repeatedly hiding.
  • Breeze has cast detect thoughts and discerns that she’s driven by fanatical devotion and is otherwise calm and prepared to die for her god.
  • Rafross grapples her, but she throws down smoke bomb that knocks out Ivir and Rafross.
  • Anwynn has levitated and is hovering near the ceiling and watches as she flees out that gatehouse.
  • Anwynn uses minor illusion to create a voice of a Kishar priest calling out for her help to escape, emitting from a pile of bodies.
  • This convinces her, and she immediately places highest priority on finding and helping the priest.
  • After luring her back within range, Ivir misses with some thrown rocks.
  • Breeze incapacitates her with Tashas Hideous Laughter.
  • Rafross grapples her, she’s incapacitated, grappled, and tied up with rope with a DC 23 check on the quality of the knots/bindings.
  • Ivir walks over and kicks her, knocking her out of the laughter, levels the molten glowing glaive at her throat and asks “where is Ursula, what did you do to her!? Why did you do this? We were ready to help you and your people and you betrayed us!”
  • She says in response “What could you do that a god can’t”
  • Breeze has detect thoughts and picks up “Who the fuck is Ursula” then confusion because it doesn’t compute that her people need help or are danger at all.
  • Breeze shouts out to Ivir that “she doesn’t remember the bear”
  • Ivir knocks her with the butt of the glaive and says “The bear I sent with you”
  • Breeze detects her thoughts as “why would I keep that around” and that she didn’t kill the bear, but her being confused that he would care more about an animal than anything else. Also picks up that she killed the human spies she was sent to communicate with.
  • Rafross takes the holy symbol away from her, and as he does she says incredulously “you fools, did you really expect this to be ‘proof’ of anything”
  • Rafross retrieves two potions of pocket sand (blinding), one essence of ether (unconsciousness), four poison darts (torpor) + one no longer poisonous dart from his neck.
  • Breeze asks if her people are fanatics as well.
  • He picks up on her thoughts of her counting in her mind, because she’s realised that he’s detecting her thoughts and she’s trying to counter it.
  • He pushes deeper, and she knows that he’s pushing deeper into her thoughts.
  • He catches that no the whole village aren’t fanatics, but gets the image of the faces of four gnomes but no names.
  • Ivir questions Kishars power, tells her that we are more powerful.
  • She responds “She will free our world, she is the shield that will break the way open to new worlds”
  • Breeze picks up that she’s completely contemptuous of these questions and nothing is coming close to questioning her devotion.
  • Annwyn asks Ivir to leave to go search for Ursula while we wait for reinforcements to take her prisoner.
  • Annwyn also mentions what Kishar said about being part of Kishars brother and maybe that’s useful.
  • Breeze picks up on her thoughts about “Kishars sibling, Anshar, the reflection and complete opposite. Kishar is the earth and Anshar is the Horizon. Anshar is the barrier to leave for Kishar”
  • Anshar is a barrier in the way of leaving the earth, but she doesn’t have any planar understanding.

Ivir walks off into the forest, the feeling of rage slowly leaving his body, a primal animalistic feeling of retreat to a quiet dark place. Feeling embarrassed and regretful about getting caught up in rage and attacking his friends. Feeling the weight of actually having friends and a place for the first time with exile, and that Annwyn took the care to give him a moment of relief. He takes a moment to purge the poison from his body, and preparing for the worst and anticipating the site of a massacre moves forward, speaking to the animals of the forest to get what information he can. Feelings of failing so utterly his original home and village, while simultaneously having succeeded at protecting all these people that he had so little connection to. The knowledge that fighting to let the refugees through the gate led to four fanatics being granted entry weighs heavily on him.

Walking through the forest he comes upon three of the humans killed by Elga, with signs of a fourth further into the undergrowth. Amongst them, he see’s the face of Costa, covered in grime and tears. Using the last of his strength, Ivir heals him and brings him back from the edge. Almost non-verbal, he manages to tell Ivir how much he wanted to help, how much he hated being told that it was never the right time, how he pushed himself to the brink to get here in time to help and arrived just too late to save any of the humans. Ivir holds him and apologises for failing them all, for letting them down, for trusting the wrong person and feeling responsible for these deaths.

Ivir throws Costa over his shoulder and asks if he’ll come and help look for Ursula. Costa immediately holds tightly, clinging onto him as a beacon of hope.

From the animals he learns that Urusla is fine and has gone back to her natural behaviour.

He turns his attention to digging graves for the fallen humans.
