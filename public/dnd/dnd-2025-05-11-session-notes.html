May 11, 2025 Session Notes

May 11, 2025 - Session Notes

May 11, 20257 min read

  • DnD

All at the Wall

  • Door looks very plain and nondescript.
  • Insert doorknob, roll 13 on chr arcana check.
  • Feeling of rightness and familiarity, but unable to remember where from or what exactly the feeling is.
  • Succeed on a roll to push the door open, with Anwynn subconsciously prioritising getting to their patron.
  • Door opens into the mysterious travelling house we visited.

Breeze, Rafross, and Ivir Alone at the Wall

  • Breeze decides to use summon aberration to attempt to summon Starspawn.
  • Rafross fails history check, but crits on a perception check and finds out that Breeze reached deep into some part of his soul and tore open reality, to the same place that the boggle creatures from the beginning came from.
  • Rafross gets a 17 on a wisdom save but its not enough.
  • He can see through the tear but he has such perfect vision of the tear that he can see all possible planes that the tear connects to.
  • Where this has been summoned from is like one page of the book that’s been torn open, the starspawn emerges and that plane seals behind it.
  • Rafross is so shaken that he loses the ability to talk or communicate, all ability to use language is gone.
  • Starspawn attacks the wall with a 15 roll for psychic slam and gets thrown backwards.
  • Ivir sees the Starspawn attack the wall and get thrown backwards.
  • Ivir charges up to the wall with his glaive flaming, and succeeds on an attack roll and does, dealing 19 fire damage.
  • To pull the glaive out of the wall, it feels like pulling through something very thick and heavy and a piece of the thread has pulled out.

Anwynn and Gladys

  • A tall imposing and powerful but nonthreatening woman steps into the room, with the small reptile creature tucked into a sleeping bag thing on her shoulder.
  • She sees us and is nonplussed, invites us in for tea.
  • She complements Gladys on the meal she had started cooking when we were last there.
  • She starts doing this like boiling water and steeping tea, and though they should take a lot longer, seem to happen instantly.
  • She locks the pantry and lets Berry out and she Berry immediately runs over to smother Gladys.
  • Anwynn asks what to call her.
  • She says that our friends have been calling her Babayaga.
  • I respond saying that I can use that name but it seems unlikely that Ivir would know her name.
  • She says that she is “The Patron”, a facilitator of deals.
  • All of a sudden the tea is gone, but Anwynn and Gladys are satiated.
  • She tells Gladys not to lose herself in the coming chaos of the world.
  • Anwynn asks The Patron if she can restore their memories. She says that she can’t but Anwynn is the result of a deal made with an entity that they were once a part of.
  • They ask her whether they have any regard or loyalties to the planes she exists upon, but she doesn’t, she only exists to make deals.
  • As a final question/statement Anwynn says that it was good to meet her at last, and that they hope that she doesn’t deal the world into destruction. She responds saying that Anwynn shouldn’t worry and that they’re doing fine.

All at the Wall Again

  • Rafross attacks the wall again with a 28. Gets a 19 athletics check to break the thread.
  • At this moment Gladys and Anwynn step back into the scene.
  • Rafross’ hand wraps are falling apart even more with each attack. As the thread snaps they unravel even more.
  • We all feel an otherworldly pulse of power.
  • Breeze feels thorny brambles harden around him.
  • Rafross can feel the woven wall which is only tangible to him, fall over and smother him. Not unpleasant but overwhelming.
  • After this pulse we see a 16 ft figure with scaly skin that more resembles cracked earth, the same figure as the statue underwater.
  • It’s Gaia/Kishar.
  • She seems mad.
  • “And after I gave you all such a lovely wish…”

Fight Begins

  • Rafross confidently strides up to her and punches, rolling a 25 with a stunning strike. Gaia passes the con save on stunned. Deals 13 damage regardless.
  • Attacks again with another stunning strike, does 8 damage but the stunning strike fails again.
  • Rafross only comes up to her hip at best.
  • Gaia’s turn, “really, this is what destroyed my wall, you’re all sooo small”
  • She summons an earthen hand that attacks Rafross. He just manages to push it aside and avoids its damage.
  • Anwynn tries to cast faerie fire on her but it fails.
  • Breeze’s turn and he asks why we wouldn’t want to tear the wall down. She responds “You don’t like being contained?”
  • Breeze attacks for 23 points of psychic damage, and she is forced her reaction to move directly away from Breeze.
  • Ivir hits with an opportunity attack for 18 damage.
  • She uses a legendary reaction to teleport right on top of Gladys.
  • Ivir moves in, powering up his glaive, and attacks with smite, dealing 38 damage, passes strength saving throw.
  • She seems fine.
  • Ivir feels like he ran into a wall.
  • He attacks again for another 27 damage.
  • She attacks Ivir and he takes 19 halved to 9 damage.
  • Gladys uses fire magic for 19 damage.
  • Rafross hexs her for wisdom checks.
  • Then hits for 9 force damage and 1 necrotic. Exactly the same again on opportunity attack.
  • Ivir gets opportunity 22.
  • We all have to do a consave.
  • Gladys and Rafross fail. Breeze and Anwynn pass and get to halve it. Ivir succeeds and can quarter it.
  • First half, 33 damage, halved to 16.
  • Second half 37 damage, halved to 18.
  • Gladys goes down,
  • Anwynn moves over to Gladys, then Hex’s Gaia’s strength and uses eldritch blast for 12 damage. She responds to it as if it was far more.
  • Anwynn succeeds a deception check to hide their inner thoughts from Gaia.
  • Breeze heals Gladys and attacks the wall with acid splash for 7 damage.
  • Ivir reckless attacks the wall, for 25 on the first attack and gets a thread. Rolls 26 with athletics to snap the string.

The Wall Crumbles

  • Anwynn fails a deception check against Gaia, she stares straight at them as the wall falls apart. I see recognition in her eyes.
  • Gaia stares into Breeze’s soul and says “The gifts of my father will not help you fight me”, not talking about the shards.
  • Gaia glances at Rafross’ tattered broken hand wraps and they fall apart in tatters, “You could have been so great if you hadn’t abandoned the boons given to you, good luck finding any other being to grant more to you”.
  • She turns to Ivir, taking in the flaming glaive and the power in Ivir’s hands with a look of hunger on her face. “The destruction of my mother will not protect you”. “Destruction” being the fire that consumes.
  • She looks towards Gladys but doesn’t know what to make of her. “I see the touch of my father in you but I don’t think that’s your path. We shall meet again”.
  • “You have opened the door, you cannot close it, the gifts of my father shall not bind me, and I shall free my mother.”
  • Turning to Anwynn she says “And I shall destroy my brother no matter how many parts he breaks himself into”
